The Lantern in the Snow
by Barii

Mira found the lantern after the first snow. It waited beside the path, warm
and bright, though no footprints led to it.

She carried it toward the village. With every step the familiar road revealed
something she had overlooked: a fox asleep beneath the pines, the silver line
of the river, and smoke rising from a distant chimney.

At home, Mira placed the lantern by the window. Travelers began to use its
light as a guide, and the small house at the edge of the woods became a place
where no one stayed lost for long.
